Before you use dictation

Your speech is processed by your browser, not by Bayer. Most browsers send the recorded audio to the browser vendor's speech service to transcribe it. The audio does not reach this application — only the text it returns does, and only once you send it.

Do not dictate patient-identifiable information. Ask your question in general clinical terms. Transcribed text is treated exactly like typed text: it is shown to you for review before anything is sent, and if you send it, it is recorded in the engagement audit log.

Dictation is a convenience for entering a question. It does not change what this service can answer, and every answer remains bounded to the approved Mirena® scientific documentation.
