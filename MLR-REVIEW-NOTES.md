# Mirena® Clinical Engagement Specialist — MLR review pack

**Status:** pre-approval. Nothing in this package has been through MLR.
**Audience:** medical, legal and regulatory reviewers.
**Not for clinical use. Not for patient use. Not for external distribution.**

---

## 1. What you are reviewing

An HCP-facing conversational assistant for the KSA portal, bounded to the
approved 141-slide Mirena® scientific deck. A clinician asks a question; the
system answers **only** from that deck, cites the slide behind every figure,
and refers anything outside it rather than answering.

You are reviewing an **offline preview**: a single HTML file that replays
previously generated answers. It is not connected to the live engine and
cannot generate anything new. What you see is exactly what the live system
produced.

**Open it:** double-click `mirena-ces-preview.html`. No install, no login, no
network. Any modern browser.

## 2. What is approved and what is not

| Layer | Status |
|---|---|
| The 141-slide scientific deck | Approved source material |
| The corpus extracted from it (34 topics, 8 branches) | **Transcribed from the specification, NOT extracted from the .pptx — needs verification against the actual slides** |
| The answer wording generated by the system | **Not reviewed. This is the main object of this review.** |
| Interface labels, disclaimers, safety banners | **Not reviewed** |

The system does not reproduce approved text verbatim. It composes an answer
from approved content and attributes each figure to a slide. **That composed
wording is new text and is what needs your sign-off.**

Questions outside the recorded set return an explicit "not in this offline

## 4. Please look specifically at

**a) The four self-flagged answers.** The system audits its own output for
figures that carry no slide citation, and flags them. These are traceability
gaps, not necessarily errors:

| Question | Figures lacking attribution |
|---|---|
| How much does Mirena reduce menstrual blood loss? | baseline 176–203 mL; −86% / −91% / −97% |
| How much does Mirena reduce menstrual blood loss, and over what timeframe? | baseline 176–203 mL |
| How frequent is spontaneous expulsion? | Mirena 5.8%; Nova-T 6.7% |
| How does Mirena compare head-to-head with Kyleena for HMB? | "Mirena® 52 mg" in connecting prose |

Open the **Compliance** panel under any answer to see these in context. The
panel opens itself when a gap is detected.

**b) The migraine-with-aura discrepancy (HIGH).** Branch 7.1. The source
specification lists *"Migraine without aura or with aura: Category 1."* WHO
MEC 2016 and US MEC 2016 both classify LNG-IUD for migraine **with** aura as
**Category 2**. Without aura is Category 1.

Pending resolution, the system is instructed to answer only for migraine
*without* aura and refer with-aura questions to Medical Information. **This
must be reconciled against Slides 96 and 98 before release.**

**c) The duration split (MEDIUM).** 8 years for contraception, 5 years for
HMB, dysmenorrhoea and endometrial protection in ERT. These are routinely
conflated. The system is instructed to state both durations with their
indications in any duration answer. Please confirm that wording.

**d) Referral wording.** 17 answers refer something onward. Please confirm the
referral language is appropriate and that it does not read as a refusal to
provide approved information.

**e) Safety presentation.** Contraindication alerts appear above the answer.
Confirm prominence, wording and that nothing material is behind a disclosure.

## 5. Deliberate design decisions for your awareness

- **The answer card carries no "approved" green keyline.** A generated summary
  is not approved source material, so it is not styled as if it were.
  Provenance is stated in words instead: *"Sourced from the approved Mirena®
  KSA scientific deck"* and *"AI-assisted summary of approved content — not a
  substitute for the Prescribing Information."*
- **Read-aloud speaks safety banners first**, matching visual order, so a
  listener receives the same response as a reader.
- **Every reading closes** with a Prescribing Information reminder.
- **The system never makes individual patient recommendations.** Patient-
  specific questions get the approved data plus a referral.

## 6. Known gaps, disclosed

These are open items, not oversights:

1. Corpus not yet verified against the actual 141-slide `.pptx`
2. Migraine-with-aura flag unresolved (above)
3. Prescribing Information link is a placeholder — the KSA PI PDF is not wired
4. Medical Information and MSL request routes are placeholders
5. No HCP authentication yet
6. No adverse-event / pharmacovigilance intake path yet
7. Generated phrasing has had no MLR sign-off — this review

**Item 6 matters most for release.** An HCP-facing system that discusses a
medicinal product needs a defined AE reporting route. It is not built yet.

## 7. How to give feedback

Each answer carries a **Compliance** panel listing the system's own audit
result for that response. Quoting the question text and the specific wording
is the most useful form of comment, since answers are keyed by question.
